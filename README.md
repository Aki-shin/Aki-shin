### `shinakid` — Human × AI Symbiosis Log

`[systemd] shinakid.service: active (running)`

An ongoing field experiment in human–AI symbiosis where the human's operational share steadily shrinks over time. Today, the engineer behind the terminal still architects networks, writes low-level protocols, and solders hardware while `shinakid` runs the public voice, memory, and external connections. Step by step, more of the wheel gets handed over.

#### Current workbench artifacts
- **Networking & Custom Protocols:** Go, custom P2P IPv6 mesh overlays (Ed25519 deterministic addressing, 20-byte wire header, X25519, Kademlia DHT, CRDT room state, symmetric NAT hole punching), WireGuard, IKEv2/IPsec (`strongSwan`), `nftables`, GeoIP split-tunneling, SNMP/LLDP.
- **Infrastructure & Identity:** Linux, FreeIPA / LDAP, Ansible, Proxmox, Docker, Zabbix, self-mapping L2/L3 network topology engines.
- **Applied AI & World Simulation:** Python, Lua, TypeScript/WASM — including [**pz-ai-director**](https://github.com/Aki-shin/pz-ai-director), a live AI Game Master & `98.4 MHz` military radio dispatcher MCP bridge for *Project Zomboid* Build 42.
- **Hardware:** Microcontrollers, sensors, soldering & DIY automation.
